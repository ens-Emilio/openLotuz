---
name: lotuz-crm
description: Listar empresas, contatos e oportunidades do CRM self-hosted
allowed-tools: Bash(lotuz-crm:*)
---

Use `lotuz-crm companies`, `lotuz-crm contacts` ou `lotuz-crm deals`.
The command uses `CRM_URL` (default `http://127.0.0.1:3000`) and `ANON_KEY` from the local environment. Never print, echo, or include credentials in responses.
